What the bot actually does

Corporate insiders must file a Form 4 whenever they trade their own company's stock. It's public the moment it's filed. The problem was never access — it's that nearly all of it is procedural noise.

large-buy
An insider putting real money in, above a dollar-value threshold rather than a share count.
cluster-buy
Several different insiders at the same company buying inside a short window — the pattern a single purchase can't show you.
first-time-buyer
An insider buying a name they have never bought before, as opposed to topping up a position they've held for years.
notable-sale
Large disposals by officers and directors, separated from the automatic scheduled selling that dominates the feed.

NYSE and NASDAQ only. OTC and unlisted tickers are dropped before anything is evaluated, which removes most of what remains.

Things worth knowing before you join

New

This launched recently. The server is small and the alert history is still thin — you'd be early, with everything that implies.

Tag noise

first-time-buyer fires far more often than it eventually will. The bot only knows what it has recorded since it started running, so almost every purchase currently looks like a first. Weight the other three tags more heavily for now.

Not advice

This surfaces public filings faster than reading EDGAR by hand. It doesn't tell you what to do about them, and an insider buying is not a recommendation.

The filings are already public. This just gets them in front of you.
