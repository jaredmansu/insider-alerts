## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one polished website social sharing card for
Insider Alert Bot, intended for Open Graph previews. Wide landscape approximately
1.905:1, ideally 1200 by 630 pixels. Completely new design. Flat charcoal navy
background #0c1116, acid pale lime accent #a5ef84, warm off-white type, restrained
thin gray dividers. Sophisticated editorial typography: large bold sans-serif
first headline line, elegant italic serif second line. Left aligned with generous
65px safe margins; the rightmost area can have a large quiet outlined typographic
'04' reference to Form 4, purely decorative. NO stock price charts, fake performance
numbers, fake transaction data, screen mockups, logos, icons, hands, photos,
gradients, or shadows. Exact text only: small spaced uppercase wordmark at top
'INSIDER ALERT BOT'; massive central headline on two lines 'They bought in.' then
'Now you know.' with the second line in lime; supporting line below 'Public SEC
Form 4 alerts. Delivered to Discord.'; bottom separated by a fine horizontal rule,
clear readable offer '7-day trial • No card required' and smaller line '$5/month
only if you choose to subscribe'. Fit all copy cleanly without clipping. Crisp
professional typography and strong hierarchy. This is a final marketing graphic,
not a website screenshot.
